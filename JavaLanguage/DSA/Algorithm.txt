## What is an Algorithm?

Think of it like this:

> **An algorithm is just a set of steps to solve a problem.**

That's it. Seriously.

---

### Real life example first 🍳

Imagine you want to make instant noodles. You'd follow these steps:

1. Boil water
2. Put noodles in
3. Wait 3 minutes
4. Add the seasoning
5. Eat

**That's an algorithm.** A clear, step-by-step instruction to get from a problem (*I'm hungry*) to a solution (*noodles are ready*).

---

### Now in coding 💻

When you write code, you're basically writing algorithms — telling the computer **exactly what steps to follow** to solve a problem.

**Example:** How do you find the biggest number in a list?

1. Look at the first number, assume it's the biggest
2. Check the next number — is it bigger?
3. If yes, that's the new biggest
4. Keep going until the list ends
5. Whatever you're holding at the end — that's the biggest number

Simple, right? That's an algorithm.

---

### The 3 things every algorithm needs:

| Thing | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Input** | What you start with |
| **Steps** | What you do with it |
| **Output** | What you get at the end |

---

The more you code, the more you'll realize — **programming is basically just writing algorithms.** Every function, every program, every app is just a bunch of steps solving a bunch of problems.
