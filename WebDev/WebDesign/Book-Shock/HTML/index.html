Who We Are

At our core, we believe that access to literature is a fundamental human right that should never be restricted by borders, economic status, or geographic location. Founded with the radical vision of creating a completely open, borderless digital sanctuary for readers across the globe, our platform provides instant access to millions of titles spanning centuries of human thought. We meticulously source our catalog from public domain archives, open-access research repositories, and independent authors who share our passion for universal literacy. By bridging the gap between complex digital distribution and everyday readers, we ensure that anyone with an internet connection can discover their next favorite book without a subscription, a credit card, or a paywall.

Our Mission

To democratize global education and imagination by providing free, unrestricted, and uncensored access to the world's literature. We aim to break down the financial and systemic barriers that keep knowledge gatekept, empowering students, educators, and casual readers everywhere to learn without limits.

Our Vision

To build the world's largest decentralized digital library, fostering a global community of lifelong learners. We envision a future where every book ever written is safely archived, freely translatable, and instantly accessible in even the most remote corners of the planet, preserving human culture for generations to come.

For You

Literature knows no boundaries, and neither do our digital shelves. We track and host an incredibly diverse range of genres, from classic historical fiction and contemporary poetry to cutting-edge scientific journals, fantasy epics, and local regional folklore from six continents. We actively work on translation and localization projects to support endangered languages, making global storytelling truly universal. Our platform operates on a zero-logs privacy architecture, ensuring your reading habits remain entirely your own business. Backed by a passionate international network of volunteers and community developers, we stand firmly against censorship, keeping the joy of reading open, safe, and completely free forever.
